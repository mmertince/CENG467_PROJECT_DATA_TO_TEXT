. Hallucination: Example Index 8

    Candidate: "...Ambassador... of the United States 
    to the Republic of Guinea."

    Target: "...United States Ambassador to Trinidad and Tobago."

    Reason for Failure: This is a classic hallucination situation.
    The model swapped the correct country (Trinidad and Tobago) with 
    "Republic of Guinea." The reason behind this is that
    LLMs have a "prior" knowledge bias where they confuse
    similar entities in their internal weights, 
    overriding the provided table data.

2. Semantic Misalignment: Example Index 2

    Candidate: "The total number of passing yards for the team is 947."

    Target: "In his rookie season, Hawk led with 119 total tackles."

    Reason for Failure: The model might have focused on the wrong column.
    While 947 might be a number in the table, 
    it was not the data the target sentence was interested in
    which is Total Tackles. This shows a failure in attention—the model 
    saw the numbers in the table but failed to map them 
    to the correct semantic labels.
